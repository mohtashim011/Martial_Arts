About Wing Chun Foundation Lagos
The Wing Chun Foundation Lagos maintains direct connections to authentic martial arts lineages. In a region where many claim tradition, our results speak through the discipline of our practitioners and the transformation of our communities. We empower Nigerians through rigorous training in Wing Chun, CQC, and Tai Chi, while preserving indigenous combat systems like Dambe. From our beginnings as pop-up kwoons to our current institutional partnerships, we've built a movement that values substance over ceremony.
